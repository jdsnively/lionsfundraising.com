Information about Serving Alcohol for Sodexo Live!

Alcohol License Requirements and Training Information

Indiana Alcohol Serving Requirements

Indiana Law requires all alcohol servers to have a valid liquor permit and complete Alcohol Training.

Apply for Permit Online

Choose "Volunteer Employee Permit" when applying.

Application Process

First Time Application

Apply for a new Employee permit, Volunteer Employee permit, or Restricted (19 & 20 year-old) permit.

Renewal

Renew existing permits up to 90 days before expiration. Login with your Permit Number and last 4 digits of your SSN.

After Processing

Print your receipt immediately. You can work with the receipt for 90 days while waiting for your permit to arrive by mail.

Required Training

Complete Server Training within 120 days of permit issuance.

Complete Training Online

Print your Training Certificate when complete.

Lucas Oil Stadium Requirements

🚨 Required for Check-in

You must bring all three items to work at Lucas Oil Stadium:

• Photo ID
• Valid Alcohol Permit (or receipt)
• Training Certificate
