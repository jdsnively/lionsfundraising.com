Volunteer Guide

Lucas Oil Stadium Information

Essential guidelines for working fundraising events at LOS

You must complete the NPO Playbook before working at LOS

  1. Review all content and watch all embedded videos in the NPO Playbook
  2. Complete the Exit Survey on the final page and check "send me a copy of my responses"
  3. Forward your confirmation email to Jason Snively at fundraising@lionssports.club
ACCESS NPO PLAYBOOK

What to Bring

Required documentation. Printed copies only; phone screens are not accepted.

  • Valid state-issued picture ID (required for all volunteers)
  • 16-year-olds without state ID: school ID with a copy of their birth certificate
  • Liquor license (if applicable), printed copy
  • Training certificate (if applicable), printed copy

Bags: You may bring a small purse, clutch, or belt bag no larger than 4.5" x 6.5", or a clear stadium bag 12" x 6" x 12" or smaller. Non-compliant bags will require a trip back to your car.

What to Wear

You will be wearing your Lucas Oil Stadium shirt that was previously provided. If you do not have one yet, a shirt will be issued.

  • Black pants. Comfortable and professional. No workout pants, yoga pants, leggings, or jeans.
  • Lucas Oil Stadium shirt. Previously provided; new volunteers will receive one.
  • All black shoes. Plain black athletic shoes.
  • Hat. Bring yours if you have one, otherwise one will be provided.
  • Apron. Provided. It must always be worn inside the booth and removed when leaving the stand.

Fines are assessed for being out of compliance. Leggings, jeans, and sweatpants are not acceptable and you will be sent home.

Parking

Arrive downtown early to find free street parking near Lucas Oil Stadium. Paid lots are also available. Carpooling with other volunteers is strongly recommended. Allow extra time for parking and walking to check-in.

Arriving and Check-in

Enter through the ICC and LOS connector tunnel entrance, located on the northwest corner of South Street and Capitol Avenue. This is directly across the street from the north end of Lucas Oil Stadium. Look for the brick building that says "Indiana Convention Center."

GPS address: 200 W. South Street

Open in Maps

Landmark: If you walk to the Peyton Manning statue and look north, you will see the connector tunnel entrance. This is the only entrance we use.

Check-in is promptly at the designated time. When you walk in the doors, check-in is located to the left in the hallway. Complete check-in before going down the escalator.

Our Stand, Stand 124

Lions Sports Club is assigned to Stand 124 (Indy Doghouse). After checking in, head through the connector tunnel, go through the doors at the end of the hallway, and take the main escalator up. At the top, turn left and walk until you reach Stand 124. Jason will meet you at the stand.

Stadium Rules

Violations of these rules can result in Lions being terminated from fundraising at LOS. Follow all instructions carefully.

  • No going into the stadium area at any time
  • Do not sit in the concourse during the event
  • No photography or video recording
  • Do not take any giveaways from tables. They are for paying guests only.
  • No eating in the stand
  • Drinks are allowed on the grease trap only, nowhere else in the stand
  • Everyone must be ID'd for alcohol purchases. No exceptions.
  • Wear your PPE at all times and check temperatures of every batch

Food: A meal is provided after check-in before your shift starts. You may bring your own snacks in your clear bag, and personal drinking containers are permitted.
