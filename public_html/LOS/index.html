Volunteer Guide

Lucas Oil Stadium Information

Essential guidelines for working fundraising events at LOS. Updated October 2026: read all of it and sign the agreement at the bottom before your next event.

Read this first: Sodexo is enforcing every rule, starting now

Sodexo's NPO management has told every group that volunteers are breaking the rules and that it will no longer overlook it. Their leadership walked the stands and saw a volunteer eating meatballs out of a cup, and another eating popcorn with bare hands, wiping them on a shirt, then offering to serve. More than 30 groups are waiting for a place in this program. One volunteer's mistake can cost Lions ours.

Any of these gets you banned on the spot. Sodexo designates you Do Not Return, and you are not allowed back:

  1. Eating anything inside the stand
  2. Taking anything out of the stadium that you were not given at check-in to keep, including food and giveaways
  3. Leaving through any exit other than Northwest Gate 5

Every dress code violation is a ticket and a $50 fine. Arrive without a clear bag or without ID and you are sent home, which leaves our stand short and can bring a staffing fine on the club.

Every volunteer, new or returning, signs the Lions volunteer agreement at the bottom of this page before their next event. No signature, no shift.

What to Bring

Bring a valid ID to every event. Without one you are denied entry and sent home. Documents must be printed copies; phone screens are not accepted.

  • Valid state-issued picture ID (required for all volunteers)
  • 16-year-olds without state ID: school ID with a copy of their birth certificate
  • Liquor license (if applicable), printed copy
  • Training certificate (if applicable), printed copy

Bags: The only bag allowed is a clear NFL stadium bag, 12" x 6" x 12" or smaller, and any food you bring from home goes in it. Arrive without one and you will not be let in; you will be sent home. Not having driven is not an exception, so leave every other bag at home.

What to Wear

Arrive dressed to this standard at every event. Sodexo checks every stand, and each violation is a ticket and a $50 fine.

  • Black pants. Black dress pants; black Dickies or cargo pants are also fine. No leggings, sweatpants, shorts, jeans, yoga pants, or athletic wear.
  • Lucas Oil Stadium shirt. Previously provided; new volunteers will receive one.
  • Black shoes. Plain black shoes, or approved black shoe covers put on as soon as you arrive.
  • Hat or visor, bill facing forward at all times. Solid black with no logo, or one with the Lions logo. Sodexo Live hats and visors are not allowed and carry a $50 fine. Bring yours if you have one; otherwise one will be provided.
  • Full-body black apron. Worn at all times inside the stand and removed when you leave it. Waist aprons are not accepted, and the aprons Miss Judy handed out before may no longer be used. If you do not have one, tell Jason before the event.
  • Hair. If it reaches your shoulders, tie it back in a ponytail or otherwise secure it. A hairnet does not replace your hat.

Leggings, jeans, sweatpants, and shorts get you sent home.

Parking

Arrive downtown early to find free street parking near Lucas Oil Stadium. Paid lots are also available. Carpooling with other volunteers is strongly recommended. Allow extra time for parking and walking to check-in.

Arriving and Check-in

Enter through the ICC and LOS connector tunnel entrance, located on the northwest corner of South Street and Capitol Avenue. This is directly across the street from the north end of Lucas Oil Stadium. Look for the brick building that says "Indiana Convention Center."

GPS address: 200 W. South Street

Landmark: If you walk to the Peyton Manning statue and look north, you will see the connector tunnel entrance. This is the only entrance we use.

Check-in is promptly at the designated time. When you walk in the doors, check-in is located to the left in the hallway. Complete check-in before going down the escalator.

Be checked in by the check-in time Jason gives you. If you are running late or cannot come, text Jason at (317) 796-8048 as soon as you know. A missing volunteer leaves our stand short, and Sodexo can fine the club for it.

Our Stand, Stand 124

Lions Sports Club is assigned to Stand 124 (Indy Doghouse). After checking in, head through the connector tunnel, go through the doors at the end of the hallway, and take the main escalator up. At the top, turn left and walk until you reach Stand 124. Jason will meet you at the stand.

Following Sodexo Staff

When any Sodexo employee, supervisor, or helper gives you an instruction, follow it right away and respectfully. No arguing and no back talk.

If you think an instruction is wrong, follow it anyway and tell Jason afterward; he will take it up with Sodexo. How we respond in the stand is part of how Sodexo decides whether Lions keeps its place. Violations of any rule on this page can end Lions' fundraising at Lucas Oil Stadium.

Food Safety

  • No eating inside the stand, ever. Sodexo designates anyone caught Do Not Return.
  • Never touch food with bare hands. Wear your gloves and PPE at all times, and never wipe your hands on your clothes.
  • Check the temperature of every batch.
  • Drinks are allowed on the grease trap only, nowhere else in the stand.
  • Never take food from the stand without paying for it. Sodexo treats it as theft.

Your meal: A meal is provided after check-in, before your shift starts, and food provided from Pantry 108 is allowed. Once Pantry 108 closes, any food you buy needs its receipt with you as proof of purchase. Food from home must be carried in your clear bag, and personal drinking containers are permitted.

During the Event

  • Stay at Stand 124 for the whole event. No wandering the stadium or visiting other levels or stands.
  • Step away only for a short break, the restroom, a short smoking break, or to get a shirt from Miss Judy. 15 minutes at most, then come straight back.
  • No going into the stadium seating area at any time, and do not sit in the concourse during the event.
  • No photography or video recording.
  • Everyone must be ID'd for alcohol purchases. No exceptions.

Leaving the Stadium

Leave only through Northwest Gate 5, where the Welcome Back meetings were held. Sodexo staff are stationed there, and leaving by any other exit means Do Not Return.

  • Take nothing out of the stadium except an item you were given at check-in to keep. No food and no giveaways.
  • Do not take giveaways from the tables at all. They are for paying guests only.

If a Rule Is Broken

Lions holds every volunteer to these rules because Sodexo holds the club to them.

  • Missing an event you signed up for without telling Jason first is a violation. It leaves our stand short.
  • You repay any fine your actions cost the club, including a staffing fine when you do not show, in full, before you work another event.
  • A second violation ends your eligibility to work Lions fundraising events.
  • A Do Not Return from Sodexo ends it immediately.

Volunteer Agreement

By signing, you confirm that you have read this entire page and will follow every rule on it and every instruction from Sodexo staff. This is the Lions agreement; it is separate from Sodexo's NPO Playbook and exit survey, which you still complete. Every volunteer signs before their next event, including returning volunteers. Volunteers under 18 sign with a parent or guardian. You and Jason each receive a signed copy by email.
