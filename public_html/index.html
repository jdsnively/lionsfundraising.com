How it works

1

Get on the roster

One form, once a season. Register everyone in your family who might work, including your athlete if they are sixteen or older.

2

Pick your dates

The Lucas Oil calendar runs August through April. Colts games, concerts, band championships, the Combine. You choose the dates that fit your family and nothing else.

3

Work and get paid

You run a stand alongside other Lions families. Most crews arrive about three hours before gates and finish once the crowd clears.

What you earn

95%

Of the stand payment goes to the crew

Everyone working a shift is paid the same hourly rate. It comes from what the stand is paid for that event, divided by the hours the crew actually worked, so a busy night pays better for everybody on it.

Lions keeps five percent to run the program, and gives it up when the event is quiet. If the hourly rate would come in under twenty dollars, the club takes nothing and the crew is paid the whole amount.

Earnings are credited toward your athlete's season. You can follow the running total from your account at any time.

What you need

  • To be sixteen or older on the day you work
  • Registration with Lions Sports Club, which is the form below
  • A state permit and Sodexo alcohol training, but only if you want to serve alcohol. Most positions do not.
