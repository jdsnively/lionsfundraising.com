About Lions Football Club

23 Years Of Building A Winning Tradition

The Lions Island School Football Club is a program, not for profit organization where kids have the opportunity to learn the game while giving themselves an opportunity to participate in competitive football.

We have developed young men into mature and trained athletes, building character and developing godly young men and women from all over Indiana.
